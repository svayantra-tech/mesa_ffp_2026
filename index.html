How they pitched

DEMO DAY to Tier-1 VCs

Students took the stage to pitch their ventures to venture capitalists from Tier-1 VCs — defending their numbers, business models, and growth strategy.

Every team had 5 minutes to pitch, 3 minutes for Q&A. Judges scored on revenue, presentation quality, and growth strategy. Winners earned recognition across the entire cohort.
Demo Day wasn't a school presentation — it was a real pitch to real investors. Students defended revenue numbers, customer acquisition costs, and margins under live questioning. The best teams walked away with awards and the confidence that their business actually works.

INTERESTED in FFP 2027?

The Future Founder's Summer School returns next year. If you're a student, parent, or educator — find out how to apply for the next cohort.

FFP is a 2-week intensive entrepreneurship programme by Mesa School of Business, Bangalore. Students form teams, build real products, sell to real customers, and pitch to real investors — all before they turn 18.
